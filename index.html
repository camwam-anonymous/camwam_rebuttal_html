Closed-loop results in 3DGS scenes

Selected examples of CamWAM-1.3B on our closed-loop benchmark. In each round, CamWAM predicts a short future video together with camera actions; the first 8 actions are executed in the 3DGS scene, the new view is observed, and the model replans. Execution stops automatically once the predicted motion becomes small (at least 2 and at most 10 rounds).

Real-world experiments

To check whether CamWAM transfers from rendered scenes to real camera footage, we built a human-in-the-loop setup during the rebuttal period.

  1. The iPhone streams camera frames and its 6-DoF pose (from ARKit) to a Mac.
  2. The Mac forwards the current frame to a GPU server running CamWAM, which predicts a future video and the corresponding camera actions.
  3. The actions are converted into a target camera pose in the ARKit world frame and sent back to the Mac.
  4. The Mac displays the remaining 6-DoF Δpose (translation and rotation) to the operator until the phone reaches the target pose (within 10 cm / 5°); the next frame is then captured.

We present four outdoor demonstrations using a handheld iPhone, showing CamWAM-1.3B's predicted future videos alongside real images captured after executing the predicted camera movements. These examples provide preliminary validation of CamWAM in real-world settings during the rebuttal period.
